Rudolfstetten-Friedlisberg AG

Generous, sustainable living on the edge of the forest

On the Isleren site a quarter of 17 staggered houses is taking shape between the Mutschellen junction and the forest: 278 rental apartments, a square under birches, and not a single car above ground.
