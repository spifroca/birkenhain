Sustainability & energy

Density where the infrastructure already is

Instead of new building land at the edges, the quarter uses a key site inside the existing settlement, ten minutes from the railway. That is the most effective sustainability measure in the whole project.

An energy concept secures the supply: photovoltaics on the roofs, extensive green roofs, rainwater retention and infiltration on site, ecological compensation areas, and buildings aligned with the cold-air corridors.

The energy concept

Energy concept Real North AG, 15 July 2025

Heat

Ground-source heat pump

Heating and hot water come from boreholes — around 22,000 m of probes for an estimated demand of 1,000 kW. In summer the same field cools the flats passively through the underfloor circuit (freecooling).

Electricity

2,900 m² of photovoltaics

Each building has its own inverter. Solar power serves heating, cooling and hot water first; the surplus goes to residents through a ZEV self-consumption group or into the grid.

Storage

1–3 MWh battery

A central battery room is planned to raise self-consumption, flatten peak loads and stabilise the local grid — subject to proven economic viability.

Envelope

Minergie-aligned

Built to Minergie level without certification: heavy insulation, triple glazing, an airtight envelope, underfloor heating with room-by-room control — 21 °C living, 23 °C bathrooms.

Examined and set aside

Wood-chip heating — the forest is next door, but winter demand would mean a delivery every four to six days: too much grey energy and noise.

District heating — AEW is extending its network in Rudolfstetten, but neither costs nor connection date are fixed, so it is not pursued for now.

Air-source heat pumps — lower efficiency at low temperatures, more noise, more space; ground probes win the comparison.

And in the flats

Windows that open: no mechanical ventilation system in the flats, only enclosed rooms are extracted mechanically.

Kitchens with recirculation hoods and activated-carbon filters.

The natural pool stays unheated; whether it can act as a buffer in the energy system is being examined.

Charging points in the garage, prepared for expansion, with load management to prevent overloads.

The fifth façade: photovoltaics where the sun is best, extensive planting everywhere else.

First letting follows — be on the interest list before it does.
