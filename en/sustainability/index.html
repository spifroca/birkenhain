Sustainability & energy

Density where the infrastructure already is

Instead of new building land at the edges, the quarter uses a key site inside the existing settlement, ten minutes from the railway. That is the most effective sustainability measure in the whole project.

Energy concept

  • Ground-source heat pump

    22'000m Sondenlaenge

    Heating and hot water come from boreholes, sized for an estimated demand of 1,000 kW.

  • Photovoltaics

    2'900m2 Dachflaeche

    One inverter per building. Solar power serves heating, cooling and hot water first; the surplus goes to residents through a self-consumption group.

  • Battery storage

    1–3MWh

    A central battery room is planned: more self-consumption, flattened peak loads, a more stable local grid. Subject to proven viability.

  • Free cooling

    In summer the same borehole field cools the flats passively through the underfloor circuit.

  • Building envelope

    Aligned with the Minergie standard but not certified: heavy insulation, triple glazing, an airtight envelope, underfloor heating with room-by-room control (21 C living, 23 C bathrooms).

  • Green roofs

    Extensive planting on every flat roof not used for photovoltaics or terraces.

  • Retention and infiltration

    Surface water stays on site: infiltration and planted layers instead of drainage.

  • Charging points

    Charging points in the underground garage, prepared for expansion, with load management to prevent overloads.

  • Inner development

    180E/ha

    The quarter uses a key site inside the existing settlement instead of new building land at the edges. At most 70 per cent of the area is built over or under, 61 per cent in the guiding project.
